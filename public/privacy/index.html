Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. Information We Collect

When you register, we collect:

  • Your chosen username (and therefore email address)
  • The date and time of registration

We do not collect your IP address, browser information, or any other personal data beyond what is necessary to create your email account.

2. How We Use Your Information

Your username is used solely to create your email account with our infrastructure provider (MXRoute). We do not use it for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose.

3. Email Content

We do not read, scan, analyze, or process the contents of your email messages. Your email is stored on MXRoute infrastructure and is subject to MXRoute's privacy policy.

4. Data Sharing

We share your username with MXRoute solely for the purpose of creating and maintaining your email account. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with any other third parties.

5. Cookies

This website does not use cookies, tracking pixels, or any analytics tools. We do not track your usage of this website.

6. Data Retention

Your account and associated email data is retained as long as your account is active. If your account is terminated (by you or by us), your data may be deleted within 30 days.

7. Security

Passwords are never stored by us — they are passed directly to MXRoute at the time of account creation. We do not have access to your password after registration.

8. Your Rights

You may request deletion of your account at any time by contacting us at admin@hostyouremail.com. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.

10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, contact us at admin@hostyouremail.com.
