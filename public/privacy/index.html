Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 1, 2025

1. What we collect

When you register, we collect only your chosen username (which becomes your email address). We don't collect your IP address, browser information, or any other personal data.

2. How we use it

Your username is used solely to create your email account. We don't use it for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose.

3. Email content

We don't read, scan, or process the contents of your email messages.

4. Data sharing

We don't sell, rent, or share your data with any third parties.

5. Cookies and tracking

This website uses no cookies, tracking pixels, or analytics tools.

6. Passwords

Your password is never stored by us. It is passed directly to our infrastructure at the time of account creation and is not retained after that.

7. Data retention

Your account data is retained while your account is active. Terminated accounts may be deleted within 30 days.

8. Your rights

You may request deletion of your account by contacting admin@hostyouremail.com. We will process requests within 30 days.

9. Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted here with an updated date.
